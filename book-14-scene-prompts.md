# Book 14 正式插图提示词

## 生成方式

- 模式：`imagegen`（故事插画 / historical scene）
- 每张图的完整提示词 = 下方「统一前缀」+ ` Scene: ` + 对应的「场景提示」。
- 30 张图均以独立调用生成，生成后统一转换为 1600×900 WebP。

## 统一前缀

Create a polished 16:9 landscape storybook illustration in warm classic gouache and watercolor, with soft paper texture, cinematic natural light, expressive but realistic faces, and historically grounded Qin-dynasty China circa 200 BCE. Keep the recurring children consistent: Jack is an 8-year-old boy with tousled dark hair and round black glasses; Annie is a 7-year-old girl with a brown ponytail. In ancient China they wear loose woven tunics and trousers, straw shoes, and round cloth caps. Culturally respectful, adventurous and suitable for children ages 7–10. No text, no letters, no captions, no logo, no watermark, no frame, no modern objects, no gore, no visible injury.
